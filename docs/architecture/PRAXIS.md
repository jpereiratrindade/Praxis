# Praxis — Governed Operational Harness

**Praxis implements a Governed Operational Harness (HOA).**

The change from SisTer-HOA to Praxis is ontological rather than cosmetic: the operator is no longer modeled as a component of the SisTer ecosystem, but as a general instrument for governed experimentation over selected workspaces.

## Separation of identities

- **Praxis** — product and executable tool.
- **HOA** — architectural category: Governed Operational Harness.
- **workspace** — operational boundary selected at runtime.
- **SisTer** — one possible system/workspace operated by Praxis.

## Core invariant

Praxis does not know the system in advance. Praxis knows a workspace capable of declaring a system.
