# Praxis identity policy

Praxis is the product identity. HOA (Governed Operational Harness) is the architectural concept implemented by Praxis.

Current operational artifacts MUST use neutral workspace vocabulary unless they are explicitly SisTer adapters or historical records.

Compatibility names (`hoa`, `sister-ops`, `SISTER_HOA_HOME`, `.sister-hoa`) may be read during migration but MUST NOT be the canonical path for new state or documentation.
